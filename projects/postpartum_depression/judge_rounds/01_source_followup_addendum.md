# Additional PPD prior-art constraint from a 2025 paper

The [2025 Frontiers in Endocrinology article](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/endocrinology/articles/10.3389/fendo.2025.1542327/full) reports an exosome-related gene diagnostic model, drawing its expression data from the same GSE45603 cohort used by this project. It states a 43-sample subset (16 PPD, 27 controls), whereas our source-audited expression comparison uses 48 samples (16 PPD, 32 euthymic/control). The five-control difference and phenotype filtering must be resolved before any same-task comparison; matching GSE accession does not mean identical people. The 2025 paper identifies a ten-gene logistic signature using GSE45603 and evaluates its risk score on that same series. Its exosome-related claim is therefore prior art, not a novel PPD direction, and it does not furnish an independent validation cohort for ours. We did not compare scores or open new expression outcomes. A future benchmark analysis would need exact patient inclusion, train/test split, preprocessing and metric parity and an untouched external cohort.

This paper also shows why a general "exosome-related PPD signature" pivot is too close to published work. The open PPD gap remains an independent donor-resolved source and positive same-task comparison plus an actual new finding; one ChatGPT critique is logged, not ten.
