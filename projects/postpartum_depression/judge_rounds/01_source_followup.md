# Round 01 source triage, not outcome analysis

The ChatGPT response's top-ranked mechanism/pathway pivot was checked against primary and archive sources before adopting it. There is not yet a viable untouched validation chain.

- [2021 Translational Psychiatry MMI study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7933180/) already reported immune/neutrophil, innate-immunity and fibrinogen pathway findings on 137 third-trimester blood transcriptomes (15 later PPD, 122 non-PPD), with the outcome assessed two months postpartum. Thus "immune pathway in PPD" alone is not a new discovery. We did not find a GEO deposition or individual-level data access in the inspected paper; this absence is not proof no data exist. Its prospective endpoint is different from postpartum concurrent GSE290313.
- [2018 Scientific Reports PBMC paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-018-28509-4) studied 56 PPD and 27 controls, but its genome-wide discovery comparison itself used eight cases and eight controls; other measurements include qPCR/ELISA. Its PBMC compartment differs from whole blood. The inspected article does not establish a retrievable person-level transcriptome accession; this is a source lead, not a holdout.
- [GSE290797](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE290797) is 92 *pregnancy* whole-blood microarrays: 41 currently depressed, 23 with depression history only, 28 never depressed. It is not postpartum-onset depression and cannot be relabeled as PPD validation. It could only support a separately registered antenatal-depression specificity question, requiring clinical/publication checks.

No expression values opened. No accession increment, pre-value lock, selected pivot or benchmark win. Next source step: targeted search for a distinct public postpartum-onset cohort with person-level metadata and a published same-task comparator. If the independent cohort is unavailable, seek depositor clarification or reformulate the question with a genuinely distinct defensible endpoint, not an already-published immune-pathway rediscovery.
