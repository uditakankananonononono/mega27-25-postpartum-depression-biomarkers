# GSE45603 comparator-source and prior-art notes

Checked September 27, 2026 against individual GEO records, the series source and the 2014 Cambridge publisher page.

- GEO series: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE45603 . The 48 selected postpartum people include 16 labeled `condition: PPD`, 27 `condition: euthymic`, and 5 `condition: control`; source-record URLs and hashes are in `../sources/GSE45603_used_sample_crosswalk.csv`. The five generic-control GSMs are GSM1110484, GSM1110493, GSM1110498, GSM1110515, GSM1110539. These are 5 distinct source person tokens. The label does not itself establish or rule out clinical euthymia; do not upgrade it.
- Publisher's 2014 paper: https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/psychological-medicine/article/early-predictive-biomarkers-for-postpartum-depression-point-to-a-role-for-estrogen-receptor-signaling/E46434ABB857CA8F8553758AC9981925 . The accessible publisher abstract describes 62 women with psychopathology in a longitudinal discovery cohort, first/third trimester and early postpartum expression, and a third-trimester 116-transcript PPD-versus-euthymic prediction with reported 88% accuracy in discovery and replication cohorts. This is not the same as our postpartum 16-vs-32 descriptive gene-effect analysis, and a direct accuracy comparison would be invalid. The accessible page does not provide a five-GSM clinical adjudication; that remains unknown.
- Search of source metadata and accessible publisher abstract found no verified person-level clinical definition distinguishing the five generic controls. An author-level crosswalk or clinical supplement with exact IDs is needed to settle whether they should enter a strict euthymic comparator. Until then keep both broad and literal-label sensitivity as exploratory, not selection of whichever version looks better.
- Raw GEO downloads reproduced all cached gene values exactly; see `gse45603_rebuild_audit.json`. The prior pipeline selected max-mean probes using all 210 longitudinal samples before taking postpartum columns. It must not be treated as fold-isolated prospective feature selection.

No patient-level external validation, independent novel marker or published same-task benchmark win is established by this work. No judge consultation is counted for this source audit.
