# GSE45603 broad-versus-euthymic control sensitivity

Run: `python3 projects/postpartum_depression/sensitivity/gse45603_control_scope.py --matrix data/processed/postpartum_depression__GSE45603.pkl.gz` from the repo root, followed by `python3 -m pytest -q`. The processed 9,744-gene x 48-GSM matrix was copied from the shared program's previously committed analysis workspace with SHA-256 `06e9951093c1b74775f2204cb7d81afb7a754ae4d9d26f273145e6c19c555a48`. This pickle is an opaque cached gene-level artifact, not independently reconstructed from raw GEO here; source accession [GSE45603](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE45603), with each GSM record URL/hash in `../sources/GSE45603_used_sample_crosswalk.csv`. Reproducibility from the raw GPL10558 matrix remains to be checked before elevating this analysis.

The original analysis pooled 16 PPD people against 32 controls. Source labels show 27 explicit euthymic controls and five generic controls. This script holds cases and gene matrix fixed and compares standardized mean differences for broad (32) versus strict explicit-euthymic (27) controls. It does not train a classifier or alter the old published result. The two contrasts share 27 controls and all 16 cases, so their estimates are dependent. Descriptive sign flips are sensitive to near-zero effects; do not present the flip percentage as a statistically significant method or biomarker effect.

Computed output: 9,744 finite gene contrasts, 601 sign flips (6.17% of nonzero effect pairs), zero BH-FDR <0.05 genes under either scope. The broad minimum q is about 0.368, strict minimum q about 0.708. This is an exploratory phenotype-definition sensitivity on an already exposed cohort; it neither validates a new PPD biomarker nor beats a same-task published benchmark. No accession or external-service increments, no judge-round novelty credit. The five generic-control people cannot be silently relabelled euthymic.

## Raw GEO reconstruction, September 27

The previous opaque-cache hold has been narrowed. Fresh GETs of both GEO downloads matched the committed raw-file SHA-256 hashes, and `rebuild_gse45603.py` reproduces all 9,744 genes x 48 selected GSM values in the cached matrix **exactly** from the 15,456-probe x 210-GSM series matrix and GPL10558 annotation. The raw series matrix and platform annotation are preserved under `data/raw/`; source URLs, hashes and shapes are recorded in `gse45603_rebuild_audit.json`. One critical detail: the original max-mean probe for each gene was chosen using **all 210 longitudinal samples before choosing 48 postpartum samples**. That preserves the old descriptive result, but it is not fold-isolated feature construction for a prospective classifier. Re-running the gene collapse after restricting to the 48 postpartum samples changes 3,276 cells, so this ordering matters. The exploratory 601 sign flips and zero FDR findings use the exact historical gene matrix; no new predictive or independent validation claim follows.

The 601 sign reversals need scale context: only **two** genes have `|g| >= 0.1` in *both* scopes while reversing, and **zero** have `|g| >= 0.2` in both. A bare 6.17% reversal rate overstates biological instability because most effects cross zero at tiny magnitudes. The original Mehta et al. 2014 paper reports a third-trimester 116-transcript predictive analysis in a high-risk longitudinal cohort (62 women in discovery); our postpartum case-control sensitivity is a different task and not a same-task benchmark comparison: https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/psychological-medicine/article/early-predictive-biomarkers-for-postpartum-depression-point-to-a-role-for-estrogen-receptor-signaling/E46434ABB857CA8F8553758AC9981925 . GEO sample source labels alone do not adjudicate whether the five generic controls clinically met the paper's euthymia criteria.
