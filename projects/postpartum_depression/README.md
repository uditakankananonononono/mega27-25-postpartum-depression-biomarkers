# Postpartum depression - proposed separate project

Status: project scaffold and gate audit only, not a finished paper or a positive benchmark. The parent repository is shared core; this directory must hold a disease-specific protocol, accession and external-service evidence ledger, reproducible results and a 50-page substantive paper before its gates can be claimed.

Current disease-tagged expression manifest records: 170 = 2 GSE identifiers + 167 nested GSM library/sample records + 1 ontology. These are record units, not independent datasets or patients. The shared 40-service and 49-page PDF do not transfer as automatic per-project passes. Benchmark/discovery endpoint is open.

Disease-specific documents and source logs are not yet split from shared core; use the shared source code and result filenames by disease as leads, then verify original record attribution before copying.

## Separate methylation inventory, not expression-model validation

The directly fetched GEO series SOFT for [GSE335141](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE335141) lists 82 unique GSM identifiers, whole-blood EPICv2 methylation at two postpartum draws from 41 mothers (17 diagnosed PPD, 24 controls). Its source text is retained under `sources/`. A source-verified exploratory T0 beta-matrix effect screen was later run in the separate builder branch; it reports no BH-FDR<0.05 probe. That outcome has not been independently recomputed in this standalone lane, and the specimens stay outside this expression manifest. The separate methylation ledger below distinguishes analytic and source-only uses. They are two repeated samples per mother, and methylation is a different modality from the expression ranking task. A future PPD project can investigate the cohort without asserting 82 independent datasets, a same-task accuracy benchmark, or a named new marker. The [2021 PPD blood-expression publication](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41398-021-01270-5) and [2025 pregnancy/postpartum transcript study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41380-025-03068-z) supply clinical and novelty context, not newly used cohort records by themselves.

## Individually verified used postpartum whole-blood cohort

The earlier program analysis had already used [GSE45603](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE45603), but its 48 analyzed GSMs were not yet in the accession manifest. We fetched all 48 individual GEO sample SOFT records, compared exact titles, postpartum timepoint, PPD/euthymic/control condition and GPL10558 platform with the committed series matrix and label file, and checked all 48 distinct person tokens and finite expression columns. The source-URL/hash crosswalk is in `sources/GSE45603_used_sample_crosswalk.csv`; it has 16 PPD cases and 32 euthymic/control postpartum people. Those 48 expression-used GSMs were first recorded in the manifest. The separate GSE290313 P6 postpartum analysis used 119 libraries whose source records are now included as nested *library* identifiers in the expression provenance inventory; donor identity is still unresolved, so none can be counted as independent people or participant-level clinical validation. GSE335141 remains outside the expression inventory; its methylation-only screen is accounted for separately below. This is better dataset provenance, not a new positive benchmark or a novel PPD marker.

## GSE290313 postpartum library-to-person mapping pause

A separate existing P6 analysis uses 119 GSE290313 postpartum, SSRI-unexposed GSM libraries (35 labeled "depressive symptoms only postpartum", 84 controls). We independently fetched all 119 [individual GEO sample records](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE290313) and matched their title, source group, timepoint, SSRI field, GPL24676 platform and unique full library token to the committed P6 labels; source URLs and response hashes are in `sources/GSE290313_used_sample_crosswalk.csv`. **119 distinct library tokens are not proof of 119 distinct people.** The DE-number prefix repeats across records (80 distinct prefixes); some repeats are case/control discordant, for example DE21 and DE69. This prefix may be a recycled sample identifier, not necessarily a donor key. The associated [peer-reviewed paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12436180/) reports 188 women with postpartum RNA samples across its broader analysis (versus 184 GEO records explicitly titled postpartum), which makes 80 DE prefixes an implausible universal person key, but does **not** itself provide the exact donor-to-GSM crosswalk for our 119 selected libraries. Until original mapping resolves it, we do not use prefixes to collapse people or claim patient-independent validation. The 119 GSMs are individually source-verified as expression-used libraries and are now included as **nested specimen identifiers** in the per-disease provenance manifest, explicitly marked person-key unverified; the P6 expression effects remain descriptive. No reported clinical classifier or novelty result is rescued by assuming uniqueness.

## Source phenotype distinction (September 27)

The 48 GSE45603 source records divide into 16 `condition: PPD`, 27 `condition: euthymic` and 5 generic `condition: control`. Thus the earlier 16-vs-32 analysis uses a broader control pool, not 32 explicitly euthymic women. A strict euthymic contrast must either exclude the five generic controls under a registered rule or obtain source clarification. The audit now flags them; it does not change prior expression outcomes or claim a positive finding. See `transfer_audit/GSE45603_audit.json` and the source-URL/hash crosswalk.

## Separate methylation provenance ledger integrated (September 28)

The earlier shared-repo PPD builder branch had already run exploratory, different-modality methylation screens. Its source crosswalks, 139-ID ledger and code are now copied into this standalone project, **without conflating the expression and methylation manifests**. The additional ledger has two parent series and 137 GSMs, with no accession overlap with the expression manifest. It separates **91 specimens entering one-person group comparisons** (50 antenatal GSE44132, 41 delivery-time T0 GSE335141) from **46 sample records used only for mapping/quality audit** (five technical repeats in GSE44132 and 41 T4 repeated samples in GSE335141). The two GSE parents supply series and matrix provenance, not two additional independent replication cohorts for the expression task. GSE44132 contains 51 distinct source person tokens, but the comparison uses 50; GSE335141 contains 41 mothers, each T0 and T4. The earlier 51+139=190 count was an accession inventory snapshot before the 119 historically used P6 GSMs were reconciled. The current **170+139=309** count is an accession inventory across assays and nested samples, **not** 309 biological people, 309 analyzed specimens, or 309 independent studies. No same-task published benchmark score follows.

The transferred `ppd_methylation_audit.py` can download the two source matrices, check fixed SHA-256 digests and rerun exploratory tests. Its committed summary files report zero BH-FDR<0.05 CpGs in both one-person comparisons; a separate paired T4-minus-T0 exploratory screen also reports no FDR hit and T4 cannot be a prospective feature. This standalone reconciliation checked structural crosswalk/ledger invariants and live source hashes for three samples in each series, **not the 770 MB of matrix contents or numerical screens**; these remain branch-reported results, not independently replicated by this lane. Source: [GSE44132](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE44132) and [GSE335141](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE335141). The 2026 [bioRxiv GSE335141 prior art](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.04.03.716376v1) and its inconsistent clinical Table 1 denominator remain limitations. This bookkeeping is not an RNA-based marker discovery or a fair published same-task win, and the other project gates remain open.

## GSE290313 analyzed library records reconciled (September 28)

The P6 raw-count analysis had already used 119 source-matched postpartum, SSRI-unexposed [GSE290313](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE290313) GSM libraries, but those exact specimen identifiers had been omitted from the expression provenance manifest while the *person* crosswalk remained unresolved. We re-fetched all 119 full GEO sample records and verified every byte against the earlier URL/SHA-256 crosswalk; the 119 GSMs exactly equal the committed analyzed label set (35 case, 84 control). They are now recorded as **library-level used source records**, moving the expression inventory from 51 to **170 identifiers**: two parent GSE, 167 nested GSM and one ontology. This crosses only the 120 **record-unit** interpretation; it does not prove 120 independent donors or datasets. The repeated DE prefixes include conflicting case/control labels, so they are not a defensible patient key; the study's 188-woman overall paper denominator cannot fill the selected-library crosswalk. No new expression analysis, patient-independent validation, published same-task beat or biomarker follows. This provenance ledger remains separate from methylation, and the missing participant identity is a hold on clinical inference, not a reason to silently omit genuinely analyzed GSMs.
