# GSE290313 donor-key screen, September 27, 2026

The 119 selected postpartum, SSRI-unexposed GEO libraries remain 119 **specimen records**, not established as 119 independent women. Source series: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE290313 ; individually fetched GSM URL/hash crosswalk: `GSE290313_used_sample_crosswalk.csv`.

The matching [Molecular Psychiatry paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41380-025-03068-z) reports 188 women with postpartum RNA samples in its larger cohort. Its [publisher supplementary DOCX](https://media.springernature.com/original/springer-static/esm/art%3A10.1038%2Fs41380-025-03068-z/MediaObjects/41380_2025_3068_MOESM1_ESM.docx) was fetched and read: Table S3 totals 188 women (98 controls, 4 depressive-symptoms-only-in-pregnancy, 48 postpartum-onset, 38 persistent); Table S2 totals 117 pregnancy-sampled women. The supplement has aggregate participant tables and RNA processing details, **not** a GSM-to-woman identifier table. These numbers cannot be substituted for our selected 35 case/84 control library labels.

A direct source spot-check of a conflicting `DE21` prefix illustrates the issue: [GSM8810442](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSM8810442) is `DE21NGSUKBR129193`, postpartum-onset-symptom label, linked to BioSample SAMN46984903; [GSM8810586](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSM8810586) is `DE21NGSUKBR129096`, control label, BioSample SAMN46984759. Their BioSample pages carry group and timepoint but no shared donor identifier: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/biosample/SAMN46984903 and https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/biosample/SAMN46984759 . Different BioSample accessions do not prove different women, while shared `DE21` prefix with conflicting labels prevents using that prefix as an unambiguous donor key. The source group also measures symptom trajectory, not the identical DSM-5 phenotype in GSE335141.

The supplement narrows the known population denominator but does not resolve the selected-library person crosswalk. Keep `participant_independent_transfer_eligible = false` in the transfer audit. No accession or external validation credit arises from reading a supplement; a source-signed sample-to-person mapping or exact donor table is still needed. Do not request or expose private participant information as a workaround.
