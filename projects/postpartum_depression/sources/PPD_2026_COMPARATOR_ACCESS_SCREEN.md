# PPD comparator access screen, September 27, 2026

This screens public comparators for the stated PPD whole-blood biomarker objective; it is not an analysis of their outcomes, a study count increment, or a claim that no other source exists.

| Source | Published claim and specimen | Availability / task hold |
|---|---|---|
| [Mehta et al. 2014](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/psychological-medicine/article/early-predictive-biomarkers-for-postpartum-depression-point-to-a-role-for-estrogen-receptor-signaling/E46434ABB857CA8F8553758AC9981925), GEO [GSE45603](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE45603) | Third-trimester 116-transcript PPD prediction in a high-risk longitudinal cohort, reported 88% accuracy in discovery and replication; whole-blood array. | GEO expression public but already used; our postpartum 16-vs-32 descriptive differential effect is a different task. Original published performance is not a valid comparator number for our effect size. |
| [Guintivano et al. 2022](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41380-022-01525-7) | Whole-blood RNA-seq six weeks postpartum, TWAS and cell-type-specific analyses. | Publisher says full transcriptomic/DNA-methylation/SNP data have or will be deposited in dbGaP phs002103.v1.p1 with embargo. The publication's old `/projects/gap/...` URL redirects to dbGaP home in this environment; an NCBI E-utilities `db=gap` search for `phs002103` returned zero at this check. This does **not** establish no controlled-access dataset exists. No individual data used. The published TWAS association is not a patient classifier benchmark. |
| [Mehta et al. 2021](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41398-021-01270-5) | 137 women at two months postpartum, EPDS binary cutoff and expression/immune analysis. | Paper's full text did not expose a public GSE/PRJNA accession in our screen. No source matrix used and EPDS cutoff versus source clinical definitions needs a locked comparison. |
| [Pan et al. 2018](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-018-28509-4) | PBMC RNA-seq, PPD EPDS >=13 versus controls EPDS = 0. | Different blood compartment; full accessible article screen did not identify a public raw accession. No individual data used. |

Reviewer-token link is not treated as public data. See separate access-check file. |
| [Chen et al. Aug 2026](https://www.nature.com/articles/s44184-026-00232-3) | Mendelian-randomization and multi-omic QTL study identifies SRA1 as a proposed PPD target. | Prior art for SRA1, not a source of independent individual postpartum blood expression or a same-task classifier. Public GWAS summary-statistics links do not supply patient-level postpartum RNA validation. |

**Decision:** No paper in this screen currently supplies a verified public, independent, phenotype- and assay-aligned patient-level holdout with a published same-task score that our current PPD analysis can fairly beat. This is a scoped screen, not proof none exists. Do not turn association p-values or exploratory DEG counts into accuracy benchmarks. Recheck public access/author permissions if a precise cohort emerges, preregister the comparison before seeing new outcomes, and do not silently repurpose an exposed cohort as a holdout.
