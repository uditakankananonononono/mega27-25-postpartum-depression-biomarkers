## Program use and honest decision

A local search of the PPD standalone and shared repositories found prior documented SRA1 context but no PXD050851/PXD045906 accession use. This is a bounded local exposure screen, **not exhaustive** agent-history assurance. No file was downloaded from iProX, MassIVE, Olink or SomaScan, and no new candidate was selected by us. The closest exact-time PPD plasma source is author-mined PXD050851 and has only an EPDS-risk endpoint; source file/phenotype crosswalk remains inaccessible at this check. Olink and SomaScan publications provide prior art but differ in assay, draw time or psychiatric phenotype and do not form an off-the-shelf independent holdout. A credible next experiment needs a clearly authorized accessible patient-level proteomic source, verified independent people and compatible phenotype/timing, assay analyte mapping, a dated locked rule/comparator and no test-outcome peeking. Until then, peptide/protein angle is feasible for literature nomination but **not yet feasible for a new validated PPD marker**.
