# GSE45603 same-task comparator and reused-source screen, September 27, 2026

Primary publication: [Li et al., Frontiers in Endocrinology 2025](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/endocrinology/articles/10.3389/fendo.2025.1542327/full). The paper explicitly selects **16 PPD and 27 normal postpartum mood specimens from GSE45603**, unlike the project's original broad 16+32 contrast; that matches our strict explicit-euthymic sample count (verify exact GSM set before asserting identical participants). It starts from exosome-related genes, filters differential expression in that same GSE45603 cohort and fits a ten-gene diagnostic logistic risk score; the authors report apparent ROC AUC **0.942** on GSE45603 itself. The same article's Figure 6 caption reports 95% CI 0.872-1.000. The ten genes are TPP2, AKR1B1, CD59, PARK7, PLXNB2, HLA-B, FAH, NDST1, SCARB1 and HNRNPA2B1. This is a **closer phenotype/timepoint comparator** than Mehta 2014's third-trimester future-onset prediction, but is not an untouched independent validation result: gene selection, model fit and ROC are described on the same exposed GSE45603. A fair beat cannot compare our post hoc sensitivity effects to their apparent training ROC. Recreate the exact sample set, preprocessing, gene intersection and patient/group cross-validation, with selection entirely inside training folds, and apply both locked models to an independent matched cohort if one can be source-verified. Preserve this paper as prior art for the ten-gene panel; do not claim the genes as newly discovered.

Adjacent cohort: [PRAM-D blood-transcriptome paper](https://preview-www.nature.com/articles/s41398-025-03309-3) deposits [GSE290797](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE290797), public April 2025. GEO describes blood **pregnancy** microarrays for currently depressed or future-risk women; it is a different sampling window and endpoint from postpartum current PPD. Do not add it as a matched postpartum independent validation merely because it is another blood-expression accession.

No expression, participant-level model or additional used-accession credit was generated in this comparator screen. It narrows the method-comparator search, not the scientific completion gate.
